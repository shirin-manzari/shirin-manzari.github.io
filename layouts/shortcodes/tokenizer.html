    How to read the tokens

    Each color marks a token, with its ID below. Spaces appear as ␠, line breaks as ↵, and tabs as ⇥. Incomplete Unicode bytes appear as \xHH; adjacent tokens complete the character. Invisible controls appear as \u{…}. Up to 20,000 characters, processed privately in your browser.
